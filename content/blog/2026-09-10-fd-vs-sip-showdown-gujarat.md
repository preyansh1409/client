In Gujarat, generational culture prizes financial safety above all else. For decades, the default parking spot for household savings has been bank Fixed Deposits (FDs) and Recurring Deposits (RDs).

While bank deposits offer nominal safety of principal, they carry an invisible, lethal cost: **Purchasing Power Erosion**.

---

### The Mathematics of Real Return

To understand why traditional bank FDs fail to protect wealth, we must examine real post-tax, post-inflation yields:

1. **Nominal FD Interest Rate:** ~6.5% to 7.0% per annum.
2. **Taxation (30% Highest Slab + Surcharge):** Takes away approximately 2.1% in taxes, leaving you with **~4.7% net**.
3. **Lifestyle & Healthcare Inflation:** Real Indian household inflation hovers between **6.5% and 7.5%**.
4. **Net Real Return:** `4.7% - 7.0% = -2.3% per year`.

Every single year, money held in conventional FDs silently loses buying power. 

---

Over 10 to 20-year horizons, diversified equity mutual funds in India have historically generated **12% to 14% CAGR**, comfortably outperforming lifestyle inflation.

### How to Transition Safely

You do not need to plunge all your capital into high-risk equities overnight:

* **Emergency Reserves (6-9 Months):** Keep in liquid funds or bank accounts.
* **Long-Term Capital (7+ Years):** Transition gradually through Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) into goal-mapped mutual funds.
