Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about starting mutual fund services with Mr. Chetan Kumar Patel.

What is the minimum amount required to start investing?

You can begin an automated Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) with as little as ₹500 per month. For lump sum deployments, schemes typically start from ₹5,000. We customize the exact investment amount to match your monthly cashflow comfortably.

How are mutual funds safer and better than single direct stocks?

Direct stock investing exposes your capital to company-specific risk. Mutual funds pool your money into 40-70 top companies managed by full-time professional research teams and fund managers, providing massive diversification and significantly reducing volatility.

Do I have to pay any upfront fee for your distribution services?

No, our consultations, goal mapping sessions, and portfolio reviews are completely free of upfront charge. Mutual fund distributors are remunerated directly by fund houses as per SEBI regulations, ensuring complete transparency with zero hidden deductions from your invested capital.

Can I pause or withdraw my SIP investments whenever I need money?

Yes, open-ended mutual funds provide complete liquidity. You can pause or stop your SIP anytime with zero penalty, and withdraw funds directly to your registered bank account within 2-3 working days. (Only ELSS funds have a statutory 3-year lock-in for tax benefits).
