Lump Sum Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about one-time lump sum mutual fund investments.

When is lump sum investing better than SIP?

Lump sum investing is ideal when you have a large sum ready to deploy (bonus, maturity proceeds, inheritance, or property sale) and the market is at fair or undervalued levels. Historically, investing a lump sum during market corrections has generated superior returns compared to waiting on the sidelines. If you're unsure about timing, consider a Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) — where your lump sum is parked in a liquid fund and gradually transferred into equity over 3–6 months.

What is the minimum amount for a lump sum mutual fund investment?

Most mutual fund houses accept a minimum lump sum investment of ₹5,000 for equity funds and ₹1,000 for liquid/debt funds. There is no maximum limit. You can invest ₹5 Lakh, ₹50 Lakh, or even ₹5 Crore in a single transaction through your distributor. Mr. Chetan Kumar Patel can help you select the right fund category based on your investment amount and risk profile.

How are lump sum mutual fund gains taxed in India?

For equity mutual funds held for more than 12 months, long-term capital gains (LTCG) up to ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year are completely tax-free. Gains above ₹1.25 Lakh are taxed at a low rate of 12.5%. For units sold before 12 months, short-term capital gains (STCG) are taxed at 20%. Strategic partial redemptions can help you stay within the tax-free limit every year.

Can I combine a lump sum investment with a monthly SIP?

Absolutely! This is one of the most powerful strategies. Deploy your lump sum (bonus, FD maturity, etc.) immediately into a diversified equity fund, and simultaneously start a monthly SIP from your salary income. The lump sum gives you a strong compounding head-start, while the SIP builds discipline and adds rupee-cost averaging benefits on top. Mr. Patel regularly designs such hybrid portfolios for his clients.
